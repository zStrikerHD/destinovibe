# DestinoVibe — Product & Conversion Strategy

## Goal

Turn travel inspiration into qualified WhatsApp quote conversations while setting an honest expectation: every trip is tailored and availability is confirmed during service.

## Audience

People in Brazil seeking national, international and cruise travel who value a human specialist, thoughtful planning and a simple digital first contact.

## Positioning

“A viagem começa antes do embarque.” DestinoVibe combines individual attention with practical trip design. The brand voice is warm, clear and optimistic—never pushy or inflated.

## Primary conversion

A six-step guided quote request collects enough context to make the first conversation useful. Completion opens WhatsApp with a structured message; the interface explicitly says this does not mean the message was sent.

## Trust rules

- Never invent prices, availability, testimonials, volume, partners or guarantees.
- Do not publish unverified Cadastur validity or customer reviews.
- Treat CNPJ and legal copy as reviewable business/legal information.
- Label photography as inspirational and credit its source.

## Success signals

- `quote_started`, `quote_step_completed`, `quote_whatsapp_opened`
- `destination_viewed`, `contact_clicked`
- Reduced abandonment between quote steps and a healthy share of mobile completions.
